# secureflow

## 0.1.0

Docker images for commits on `main` are published as `ghcr.io/<owner>/secureflow:latest` and `ghcr.io/<owner>/secureflow:<short-sha>` (see `.github/workflows/docker-publish.yml`). Subsequent Changesets releases will list new features, database migrations, and AI flow updates under the matching `package.json` version.
